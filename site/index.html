A teammate, not a chat window.

Give an Agent a job once and it keeps doing it. It remembers what you told it, saves what it learns as skills, and runs routines on a schedule or when something happens in your tools. When it needs you — to approve a payment, sign in with a passkey, or answer a question — it stops and asks with a card you can act on.

Bring any model. Mix them.

Asylum talks to xAI, OpenAI, Anthropic, a LiteLLM proxy, Ollama (local or cloud), the Claude Code and Codex CLIs, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Every Agent can pin its own provider and model, so your researcher runs Opus while your inbox Agent runs a local Qwen — in the same group chat.

01

Their own computer

A shared workspace, a sandboxed shell, and a real Chromium the Agents drive — one screen each. Watch live, take over, or open it in a window for passkeys.

02

Ask before acting

Approvals for anything consequential, Auto-review on a fast model, and plain-language rules: "Ask before emailing anyone outside acme.com."

03

Memory that sticks

Facts, preferences, and summaries per Agent, team memory for Team Agents, and skills the Agent writes — or that you teach by demonstration.

04

Routines

Cron schedules in your timezone, intervals, webhooks, and app events from Slack, GitHub, Linear, Sentry, PagerDuty, and email.

05

Connectors

A Marketplace of MCP servers — GitHub, Linear, Notion, Jira, Sentry, Stripe, Google Workspace and more — with OAuth, multiple accounts, and per-tool switches.

06

Teams of Agents

Group chats of 2–6 Agents with @mentions, handoffs between Agents, and Team Agents your teammates add by link or meet in Slack.

07

Secrets stay secret

Values live in the macOS Keychain, are injected only into the commands that need them, and are redacted from everything an Agent sees.

08

Admin-ready

A managed policy for network controls, connector allowlists, locked rules, and execution ceilings — plus an audit log, Action Recording, OpenTelemetry, and a local Admin API.

09

Native and fast

Written in Rust on gpui with an embedded terminal from libsinclair. 31 languages, light and dark, and a keyboard shortcut for everything.

No API key? Asylum finds a local Ollama or LiteLLM proxy, and installed Claude Code or Codex CLIs, during onboarding. Nothing leaves your Mac unless your provider does.
